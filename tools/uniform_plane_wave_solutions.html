Q 01
Math · Wave Parameters
সমীকরণ থেকে ওয়েভ প্যারামিটার নির্ণয়
▼

Let the fields $\bar{E}(z,t) = 1800\cos(10^7\pi t - \beta z)\hat{a}_x$ V/m and $\bar{H}(z,t) = 3.8\cos(10^7\pi t - \beta z)\hat{a}_y$ A/m represents a uniform plane wave propagating at a velocity of $1.4 \times 10^8$ m/s in perfect dielectric. Find (i) $\beta$, (ii) $\lambda$, (iii) $\eta$, (iv) $\mu_r$, and (v) $\epsilon_r$.

Given Information

$\omega = 10^7\pi$ rad/s, wave velocity $v_p = 1.4 \times 10^8$ m/s, $E_0 = 1800$ V/m, $H_0 = 3.8$ A/m।

সমাধান

Step 1 — Phase Constant β

Perfect dielectric-এ: $v_p = \dfrac{\omega}{\beta}$

$$\beta = \frac{\omega}{v_p} = \frac{10^7\pi}{1.4 \times 10^8} = \frac{\pi}{14} \approx 0.2244 \text{ rad/m}$$
Step 2 — Wavelength λ
$$\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{\beta} = \frac{2\pi}{0.2244} = 28 \text{ m}$$
Step 3 — Intrinsic Impedance η

Perfect dielectric-এ $E$ এবং $H$ in-phase থাকে:

$$\eta = \frac{E_0}{H_0} = \frac{1800}{3.8} \approx 473.68 \text{ Ω}$$
Step 4 — μ_r এবং ε_r নির্ণয়

জানা আছে: $\eta = \sqrt{\mu/\epsilon} = \eta_0\sqrt{\mu_r/\epsilon_r}$ এবং $v_p = c/\sqrt{\mu_r\epsilon_r}$

$\eta_0 = 120\pi \approx 377$ Ω, $c = 3\times10^8$ m/s।

$$\sqrt{\mu_r\epsilon_r} = \frac{c}{v_p} = \frac{3\times10^8}{1.4\times10^8} = 2.143$$
$$\frac{\sqrt{\mu_r}}{\sqrt{\epsilon_r}} = \frac{\eta}{\eta_0} = \frac{473.68}{377} = 1.2565$$

এই দুটি সমীকরণ গুণ ও ভাগ করে:

$$\mu_r = 2.143 \times 1.2565 = 2.693 \approx \mathbf{2.69}$$
$$\epsilon_r = \frac{2.143}{1.2565} = 1.705 \approx \mathbf{1.71}$$

Q 02
Proof · Definitions
Reflection ও Transmission Coefficient — সংজ্ঞা ও সম্পর্ক প্রমাণ (1+Γ = T)
▼

Define reflection coefficient and transmission coefficient. Deduce relationship between them ($1+\Gamma = T$).

সংজ্ঞা

Reflection Coefficient (Γ): দুটি মিডিয়ামের boundary-তে incident wave-এর তুলনায় reflected wave-এর electric field-এর অনুপাতকে reflection coefficient বলে।
$$\Gamma = \frac{E_{r0}}{E_{i0}}$$

Transmission Coefficient (T): দুটি মিডিয়ামের boundary-তে incident wave-এর তুলনায় transmitted wave-এর electric field-এর অনুপাতকে transmission coefficient বলে।
$$T = \frac{E_{t0}}{E_{i0}}$$

Derivation: 1 + Γ = T

মনে করি মিডিয়াম ১ থেকে মিডিয়াম ২-তে normal incidence। $z=0$ boundary-তে:

1

Fields in medium 1 (incident + reflected):

$$E_1 = E_{i0}e^{-\gamma_1 z} + E_{r0}e^{+\gamma_1 z}$$
$$H_1 = \frac{E_{i0}}{\eta_1}e^{-\gamma_1 z} - \frac{E_{r0}}{\eta_1}e^{+\gamma_1 z}$$
2

Fields in medium 2 (transmitted only):

$$E_2 = E_{t0}e^{-\gamma_2 z}, \quad H_2 = \frac{E_{t0}}{\eta_2}e^{-\gamma_2 z}$$
3

Boundary condition at $z = 0$ — E tangential continuous:

$$E_{i0} + E_{r0} = E_{t0}$$
4

$E_{i0}$ দিয়ে ভাগ করলে:

$$1 + \frac{E_{r0}}{E_{i0}} = \frac{E_{t0}}{E_{i0}}$$
$$\boxed{1 + \Gamma = T}$$
5

H tangential continuous থেকে Γ এবং T-এর explicit expressions পাওয়া যায়:

$$\Gamma = \frac{\eta_2 - \eta_1}{\eta_2 + \eta_1}, \quad T = \frac{2\eta_2}{\eta_2 + \eta_1}$$
Key Result

Boundary condition থেকে সরাসরি প্রমাণিত: $\mathbf{1 + \Gamma = T}$। এটি এই দুই coefficient-এর মধ্যে মৌলিক সম্পর্ক।

Q 03
Derivation · Free Space
Free Space-এ Electric Field Wave Equation প্রতিপাদন
▼

Derive the wave equation for electric field in free space.

প্রারম্ভিক তথ্য

Free space-এ: $\sigma = 0$, $\rho_v = 0$, $\epsilon = \epsilon_0$, $\mu = \mu_0$। Maxwell's equations:

$$\nabla \times \bar{E} = -\mu_0\frac{\partial\bar{H}}{\partial t} \quad \cdots (1)$$ $$\nabla \times \bar{H} = \epsilon_0\frac{\partial\bar{E}}{\partial t} \quad \cdots (2)$$ $$\nabla \cdot \bar{E} = 0 \quad \cdots (3)$$

Derivation

1

সমীকরণ (1)-এর উভয় দিকে curl নিই:

$$\nabla \times (\nabla \times \bar{E}) = -\mu_0\frac{\partial}{\partial t}(\nabla \times \bar{H})$$
2

সমীকরণ (2) ব্যবহার করে ডানদিক প্রতিস্থাপন:

$$\nabla \times (\nabla \times \bar{E}) = -\mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{\partial^2\bar{E}}{\partial t^2}$$
3

Vector identity ব্যবহার: $\nabla\times(\nabla\times\bar{E}) = \nabla(\nabla\cdot\bar{E}) - \nabla^2\bar{E}$

যেহেতু $\nabla\cdot\bar{E} = 0$ (free space), সুতরাং:

$$-\nabla^2\bar{E} = -\mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{\partial^2\bar{E}}{\partial t^2}$$
Wave Equation (Free Space)
$$\boxed{\nabla^2\bar{E} = \mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{\partial^2\bar{E}}{\partial t^2}}$$

এটি wave equation। তুলনা করলে wave velocity: $v = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}} = 3\times10^8$ m/s = $c$।

📌 একইভাবে $\bar{H}$ field-এর জন্যও $\nabla^2\bar{H} = \mu_0\epsilon_0\dfrac{\partial^2\bar{H}}{\partial t^2}$ পাওয়া যায়।
Q 04
Theory & Derivation
Loss Tangent ও Skin Depth — সংজ্ঞা ও প্রতিপাদন
▼

Define the terms loss tangent and skin depth. Derive the expression of skin depth for good conductor.

Loss Tangent

সংজ্ঞা: Conduction current density ও displacement current density-এর অনুপাতকে loss tangent বলে।

$$\tan\delta = \frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon}$$

এটি medium-এর ক্ষতির পরিমাপ। $\tan\delta \gg 1$ হলে good conductor, $\tan\delta \ll 1$ হলে good dielectric।

Skin Depth

সংজ্ঞা: Conducting medium-এ wave propagate করার সময় যে দূরত্বে field-এর amplitude প্রাথমিক মানের $e^{-1}$ (≈ 36.8%) হয়ে যায়, সেই দূরত্বকে skin depth বা depth of penetration বলে।

$$\delta = \frac{1}{\alpha}$$

Good Conductor-এ Skin Depth Derivation

1

Lossy medium-এ propagation constant:

$$\gamma = \alpha + j\beta = \sqrt{j\omega\mu(\sigma + j\omega\epsilon)}$$
2

Good conductor: $\sigma \gg \omega\epsilon$, সুতরাং $\sigma + j\omega\epsilon \approx \sigma$

$$\gamma \approx \sqrt{j\omega\mu\sigma} = \sqrt{\omega\mu\sigma}\angle 45° = \sqrt{\frac{\omega\mu\sigma}{2}}(1+j)$$
3

Real part হলো attenuation constant:

$$\alpha = \sqrt{\frac{\omega\mu\sigma}{2}}$$
Skin Depth (Good Conductor)
$$\boxed{\delta = \frac{1}{\alpha} = \sqrt{\frac{2}{\omega\mu\sigma}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma}}}$$

$f$ বাড়লে $\delta$ কমে — এটি skin effect-এর কারণ।

Q 07
Theory & Derivation
Intrinsic Impedance ও Phase-Group Velocity সম্পর্ক
▼

Define intrinsic impedance. Derive the mathematical relationship between phase velocity and group velocity.

Intrinsic Impedance

সংজ্ঞা: একটি medium-এ propagating plane wave-এর electric field ও magnetic field-এর ratio-কে সেই medium-এর intrinsic impedance বা characteristic impedance বলে।

$$\eta = \frac{E}{H} = \sqrt{\frac{j\omega\mu}{\sigma + j\omega\epsilon}}$$

Free space: $\eta_0 = \sqrt{\mu_0/\epsilon_0} = 120\pi \approx 377$ Ω

Phase Velocity ও Group Velocity

Phase Velocity ($v_p$): একটি single frequency-র wave-এর constant phase point-এর velocity।

$$v_p = \frac{\omega}{\beta}$$

Group Velocity ($v_g$): Wave packet বা signal envelope-এর velocity; energy transport করে।

$$v_g = \frac{d\omega}{d\beta}$$

সম্পর্ক Derivation

1

$v_p = \omega/\beta$ থেকে: $\beta = \omega/v_p$

2

$\omega$ সম্পর্কে differentiate করলে:

$$\frac{d\beta}{d\omega} = \frac{d}{d\omega}\left(\frac{\omega}{v_p}\right) = \frac{1}{v_p} + \omega\frac{d}{d\omega}\left(\frac{1}{v_p}\right) = \frac{1}{v_p} - \frac{\omega}{v_p^2}\frac{dv_p}{d\omega}$$
3

$v_g = \dfrac{d\omega}{d\beta}$ এর inverse নিলে:

$$\frac{1}{v_g} = \frac{d\beta}{d\omega} = \frac{1}{v_p} - \frac{\omega}{v_p^2}\frac{dv_p}{d\omega}$$
Phase-Group Velocity Relation
$$\boxed{v_g = \frac{v_p}{1 - \dfrac{\omega}{v_p}\dfrac{dv_p}{d\omega}}}$$

Non-dispersive medium-এ ($dv_p/d\omega = 0$): $v_g = v_p$।
Dispersive medium-এ: $v_g \neq v_p$, সাধারণত $v_g < v_p$।

Q 08
Math · Phase Shift
Phase Shift ম্যাথ — Rangpur থেকে Chittagong (500 km)
▼

Rangpur-এ ($z=0$) $E_x = E_{x0}\cos(2\pi \times 10^8 t)$। Chittagong Rangpur-এর 500 km দক্ষিণে। $E_x$ at Chittagong নির্ণয় করো।

Phase Velocity in Free Space

Free space-এ $v_p = c = 3\times10^8$ m/s। Angular frequency:

$$\omega = 2\pi \times 10^8 \text{ rad/s}$$
Step 1 — Phase Constant β
$$\beta = \frac{\omega}{v_p} = \frac{2\pi \times 10^8}{3\times10^8} = \frac{2\pi}{3} \approx 2.094 \text{ rad/m}$$
Step 2 — Phase Shift at z = 500 km
$$z = 500 \text{ km} = 500\times10^3 \text{ m}$$
$$\beta z = \frac{2\pi}{3} \times 500\times10^3 = \frac{10^6\pi}{3} \approx 1.047\times10^6 \text{ rad}$$
Step 3 — E at Chittagong
$$E_x(z,t) = E_{x0}\cos(2\pi\times10^8 t - \beta z)$$
$$E_x\big|_{\text{Chittagong}} = E_{x0}\cos\!\left(2\pi\times10^8 t - \frac{10^6\pi}{3}\right)$$
Step 4 — Simplify the Phase

$\dfrac{10^6\pi}{3} = \dfrac{10^6}{3} \times \pi$। যেহেতু $10^6/3 \approx 333333.33$ (integer part 333333, remainder 1/3), সুতরাং:

$$\frac{10^6\pi}{3} \mod 2\pi = \frac{2\pi}{3} \text{ (since 10^6/6 has remainder } \frac{2}{3}\text{)}$$

Effective phase shift: $2\pi/3$ rad = 120°

Chittagong-এ E field
$$\boxed{E_x = E_{x0}\cos\!\left(2\pi\times10^8 t - \frac{2\pi}{3}\right)}$$

অর্থাৎ Chittagong-এ field-টি Rangpur-এর তুলনায় 120° (বা 2π/3 rad) পিছিয়ে।

Q 09
Theorem & Proof
Poynting Theorem — বিবৃতি ও প্রমাণ
▼

State and prove Poynting theorem.

বিবৃতি (Statement)

একটি closed surface দ্বারা আবদ্ধ volume-এ electromagnetic energy-র flow rate (power outflow) ঐ volume-এর ভেতরে stored energy-র হ্রাসের হার ও ohmic power dissipation-এর সমষ্টির সমান।

Proof

1

Maxwell-এর curl equations:

$$\nabla\times\bar{E} = -\mu\frac{\partial\bar{H}}{\partial t}, \quad \nabla\times\bar{H} = \sigma\bar{E} + \epsilon\frac{\partial\bar{E}}{\partial t}$$
2

প্রথম equation কে $\bar{H}$ দিয়ে dot multiply:

$$\bar{H}\cdot(\nabla\times\bar{E}) = -\mu\bar{H}\cdot\frac{\partial\bar{H}}{\partial t} = -\frac{\mu}{2}\frac{\partial H^2}{\partial t}$$
3

দ্বিতীয় equation কে $\bar{E}$ দিয়ে dot multiply:

$$\bar{E}\cdot(\nabla\times\bar{H}) = \sigma E^2 + \epsilon\bar{E}\cdot\frac{\partial\bar{E}}{\partial t} = \sigma E^2 + \frac{\epsilon}{2}\frac{\partial E^2}{\partial t}$$
4

Vector identity: $\nabla\cdot(\bar{E}\times\bar{H}) = \bar{H}\cdot(\nabla\times\bar{E}) - \bar{E}\cdot(\nabla\times\bar{H})$

Substituting:

$$\nabla\cdot(\bar{E}\times\bar{H}) = -\frac{\mu}{2}\frac{\partial H^2}{\partial t} - \sigma E^2 - \frac{\epsilon}{2}\frac{\partial E^2}{\partial t}$$
5

Volume V-তে integrate এবং Divergence Theorem প্রয়োগ:

$$\oint_S (\bar{E}\times\bar{H})\cdot d\bar{S} = -\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\int_V\!\left(\frac{\mu H^2}{2} + \frac{\epsilon E^2}{2}\right)dV - \int_V\!\sigma E^2\,dV$$
Poynting Theorem
$$\boxed{-\oint_S \bar{\mathcal{P}}\cdot d\bar{S} = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\int_V\!\left(\frac{\mu H^2}{2} + \frac{\epsilon E^2}{2}\right)dV + \int_V\!\sigma E^2\,dV}$$

$\bar{\mathcal{P}} = \bar{E}\times\bar{H}$ হলো Poynting vector (W/m²) — EM power flow density।

Q 11
Theory & Proof
Standing Wave Ratio (SWR) ও Γ-এর মধ্যে সম্পর্ক
▼

What is standing wave ratio? Establish the relationship between standing wave ratio and coefficient of reflection for plane-wave propagation.

Standing Wave Ratio (SWR)

সংজ্ঞা: দুটি medium-এর boundary-তে incident ও reflected wave-এর superposition-এ তৈরি standing wave pattern-এ maximum amplitude ও minimum amplitude-এর অনুপাতকে Standing Wave Ratio (SWR বা VSWR) বলে।

$$S = \text{SWR} = \frac{|E|_{max}}{|E|_{min}}$$

Derivation: SWR ↔ Γ

1

Medium 1-এ total E field (incident + reflected):

$$E = E_{i0}e^{-j\beta z} + \Gamma E_{i0}e^{+j\beta z} = E_{i0}\left(e^{-j\beta z} + \Gamma e^{+j\beta z}\right)$$
2

$|\Gamma| = \Gamma_0$ (magnitude), $\Gamma = \Gamma_0 e^{j\theta}$ ধরলে:

$$|E| = E_{i0}\left|1 + \Gamma_0 e^{j(2\beta z + \theta)}\right|$$
3

Maximum ও Minimum:

$$|E|_{max} = E_{i0}(1 + \Gamma_0), \quad |E|_{min} = E_{i0}(1 - \Gamma_0)$$
SWR ও Reflection Coefficient সম্পর্ক
$$\boxed{S = \frac{1 + |\Gamma|}{1 - |\Gamma|}}$$
$$\text{বিপরীতে: } |\Gamma| = \frac{S-1}{S+1}$$

Perfect match: $\Gamma=0 \Rightarrow S=1$। Total reflection: $|\Gamma|=1 \Rightarrow S\to\infty$।

Q 12
Derivation · Lossy Medium
Lossy Dielectric-এ α এবং β-এর প্রতিপাদন
▼

Derive the expression of the attenuation constant $\alpha$ and phase constant $\beta$ of a propagating wave through a lossy-dielectric medium in terms of the constituent properties.

Propagation Constant

1

Lossy medium-এ wave equation solution দেয়: $\gamma = \alpha + j\beta$

$$\gamma = \sqrt{j\omega\mu(\sigma + j\omega\epsilon)}$$
2

Expand করি:

$$\gamma^2 = j\omega\mu\sigma - \omega^2\mu\epsilon = -\omega^2\mu\epsilon + j\omega\mu\sigma$$

অতএব: $\gamma^2 = A + jB$ যেখানে $A = -\omega^2\mu\epsilon$, $B = \omega\mu\sigma$

3

$(\alpha + j\beta)^2 = (\alpha^2 - \beta^2) + j2\alpha\beta$। তুলনা করে:

$$\alpha^2 - \beta^2 = -\omega^2\mu\epsilon, \quad 2\alpha\beta = \omega\mu\sigma$$
4

$|\gamma|^2 = \alpha^2 + \beta^2 = \sqrt{(\omega^2\mu\epsilon)^2 + (\omega\mu\sigma)^2} = \omega\mu\sqrt{\epsilon^2\omega^2 + \sigma^2}$

এই দুটি সমীকরণ solve করলে:

Final Expressions
$$\boxed{\alpha = \omega\sqrt{\frac{\mu\epsilon}{2}}\left[\sqrt{1+\left(\frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon}\right)^2} - 1\right]^{1/2}}$$
$$\boxed{\beta = \omega\sqrt{\frac{\mu\epsilon}{2}}\left[\sqrt{1+\left(\frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon}\right)^2} + 1\right]^{1/2}}$$

$\sigma/\omega\epsilon$ হলো loss tangent। Perfect dielectric ($\sigma=0$): $\alpha=0$, $\beta = \omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$।

Q 13
Theory & Proof
Brewster Angle — সংজ্ঞা ও Perpendicular Polarization-এ অসম্ভবতা প্রমাণ
▼

Define Brewster angle. Show that it is not possible for a perpendicularly polarized wave.

Brewster Angle

সংজ্ঞা: Oblique incidence-এ যে angle of incidence-এ reflection coefficient শূন্য হয় (reflected wave পুরোপুরি অদৃশ্য হয়), সেই angle-কে Brewster angle ($\theta_B$) বলে।

$$\tan\theta_B = \sqrt{\frac{\epsilon_2}{\epsilon_1}} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu_1}{\mu_2}} \quad \text{(for parallel polarization)}$$

Perpendicular Polarization-এ Brewster Angle নেই — Proof

1

Perpendicular (TE) polarization-এ reflection coefficient:

$$\Gamma_\perp = \frac{\eta_2\cos\theta_i - \eta_1\cos\theta_t}{\eta_2\cos\theta_i + \eta_1\cos\theta_t}$$
2

Brewster condition: $\Gamma_\perp = 0 \Rightarrow \eta_2\cos\theta_i = \eta_1\cos\theta_t$

Non-magnetic medium ($\mu_1 = \mu_2 = \mu_0$): $\eta = \sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}$, সুতরাং $\eta_1/\eta_2 = \sqrt{\epsilon_2/\epsilon_1}$

$$\sqrt{\frac{\epsilon_2}{\epsilon_1}}\cos\theta_i = \cos\theta_t \quad \cdots (A)$$
3

Snell's law: $\sqrt{\epsilon_1}\sin\theta_i = \sqrt{\epsilon_2}\sin\theta_t$

$$\sin\theta_t = \sqrt{\frac{\epsilon_1}{\epsilon_2}}\sin\theta_i \quad \cdots (B)$$
4

(A) ও (B) থেকে $\cos^2\theta_t + \sin^2\theta_t = 1$ ব্যবহার:

$$\frac{\epsilon_2}{\epsilon_1}\cos^2\theta_i + \frac{\epsilon_1}{\epsilon_2}\sin^2\theta_i = 1$$

এটি তখনই সত্য হয় যখন $\epsilon_1 = \epsilon_2$, অর্থাৎ same medium — যা trivial case।

Conclusion

Non-trivial case-এ ($\epsilon_1 \neq \epsilon_2$) perpendicular polarization-এ Brewster angle exist করে না। Brewster angle শুধুমাত্র parallel (TM) polarization-এর জন্য সম্ভব।

Q 14
Theory & Proof
Good Conductor-এ E, H-এর মধ্যে 45° Phase Difference প্রমাণ
▼

Define characteristic impedance of a medium. Show that $\bar{E}$ leads $\bar{H}$ by $45°$ in a good conductor.

Characteristic Impedance

সংজ্ঞা: কোনো medium-এ propagating uniform plane wave-এর electric field strength ও magnetic field strength-এর ratio-কে সেই medium-এর characteristic impedance (intrinsic impedance) বলে।

$$\eta = \frac{E}{H} = \sqrt{\frac{j\omega\mu}{\sigma + j\omega\epsilon}}$$

Proof: E leads H by 45° in Good Conductor

1

Good conductor: $\sigma \gg \omega\epsilon$, তাই $\sigma + j\omega\epsilon \approx \sigma$

$$\eta_c = \sqrt{\frac{j\omega\mu}{\sigma}}$$
2

$j = e^{j\pi/2} = 1\angle90°$, সুতরাং $\sqrt{j} = e^{j\pi/4} = 1\angle45°$

$$\eta_c = \sqrt{\frac{\omega\mu}{\sigma}}\cdot\sqrt{j} = \sqrt{\frac{\omega\mu}{\sigma}}\angle 45°$$
3

সম্পর্ক: $\bar{E} = \eta_c \bar{H}$। $\eta_c$-এর phase angle 45° মানে:

$$\angle E - \angle H = \angle\eta_c = +45°$$
Conclusion

Good conductor-এ $\eta_c = |\eta_c|\angle 45°$ হওয়ার কারণে $\bar{E}$ field, $\bar{H}$ field-এর চেয়ে 45° এগিয়ে (leads) থাকে।

$$\eta_c = (1+j)\sqrt{\frac{\omega\mu}{2\sigma}} = (1+j)\frac{\alpha}{\sigma}$$
Q 15

Air-Dielectric Interface — Reflection ও Transmission Math
▼

Air ($\eta_1$) থেকে lossless dielectric ($\epsilon=3\epsilon_0$, $\mu=\mu_0$) — $\bar{E}_i = 10\cos(\omega t - z)\hat{a}_y$ V/m।
(i) $\omega$ ও $\lambda$ উভয় medium-এ। (ii) Reflection ও transmission coefficient।

Given

Medium 1 (air): $\epsilon_1=\epsilon_0$, $\mu_1=\mu_0$, $\eta_1=120\pi$ Ω।
Medium 2 (dielectric): $\epsilon_2=3\epsilon_0$, $\mu_2=\mu_0$।

From $\bar{E}_i = 10\cos(\omega t - z)\hat{a}_y$: phase constant in air $\beta_1 = 1$ rad/m।

Part (i) — ω ও λ

In Air (Medium 1)
$$\beta_1 = \omega\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0} = \frac{\omega}{c} = 1 \text{ rad/m}$$
$$\omega = c \times \beta_1 = 3\times10^8 \text{ rad/s}$$
$$\lambda_1 = \frac{2\pi}{\beta_1} = 2\pi \approx 6.28 \text{ m}$$
In Dielectric (Medium 2)
$$\beta_2 = \omega\sqrt{\mu_0 \cdot 3\epsilon_0} = \omega\sqrt{3\mu_0\epsilon_0} = \frac{\omega\sqrt{3}}{c} = \sqrt{3} \text{ rad/m}$$
$$\lambda_2 = \frac{2\pi}{\beta_2} = \frac{2\pi}{\sqrt{3}} \approx 3.63 \text{ m}$$

$\omega$ উভয় medium-এ same: $3\times10^8$ rad/s (frequency unchanged at interface)।

Part (ii) — Reflection ও Transmission Coefficient

Intrinsic Impedances
$$\eta_1 = 120\pi \approx 377 \text{ Ω}$$
$$\eta_2 = \sqrt{\frac{\mu_0}{3\epsilon_0}} = \frac{\eta_0}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{120\pi}{\sqrt{3}} = 40\sqrt{3}\pi \approx 217.7 \text{ Ω}$$
Coefficients
$$\Gamma = \frac{\eta_2 - \eta_1}{\eta_2 + \eta_1} = \frac{40\sqrt{3}\pi - 120\pi}{40\sqrt{3}\pi + 120\pi} = \frac{40\pi(\sqrt{3}-3)}{40\pi(\sqrt{3}+3)}$$
$$\Gamma = \frac{\sqrt{3}-3}{\sqrt{3}+3} = \frac{1.732 - 3}{1.732 + 3} = \frac{-1.268}{4.732} \approx -0.268$$
$$T = 1 + \Gamma = 1 + (-0.268) = 0.732$$
