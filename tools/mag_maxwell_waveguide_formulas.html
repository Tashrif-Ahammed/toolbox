1.1
Biot-Savart's Law — বায়োট-স্যাভার্ট সূত্র
Current element থেকে magnetic field বের করার মূল সূত্র
▾
General Form $d\bar{H}$
$$d\bar{H} = \frac{I\,d\bar{l} \times \hat{a}_R}{4\pi R^2} = \frac{I\,d\bar{l} \times \bar{R}}{4\pi R^3} \quad \text{A/m}$$
$\bar{R} = \bar{r} - \bar{r}'$ (field point − source point), $R = |\bar{R}|$, $\hat{a}_R = \bar{R}/R$
Total $\bar{H}$ (Line) Integration
$$\bar{H} = \int_L \frac{I\,d\bar{l} \times \hat{a}_R}{4\pi R^2} \quad \text{A/m}$$
বিশেষ geometry — shortcut
Finite Straight Wire angles $\alpha_1, \alpha_2$
$$\bar{H} = \frac{I}{4\pi\rho}(\cos\alpha_2 - \cos\alpha_1)\,\hat{a}_\phi \quad \text{A/m}$$
$\rho$ = wire থেকে লম্ব দূরত্ব। $\alpha_1, \alpha_2$ = wire-এর দুই প্রান্তের কোণ (তার বরাবর অক্ষ থেকে)।
Infinite Straight Wire $\alpha_1{=}180°,\,\alpha_2{=}0°$
$$\bar{H} = \frac{I}{2\pi\rho}\,\hat{a}_\phi \quad \text{A/m}$$
সবচেয়ে বেশি ব্যবহৃত। $\cos 0° - \cos 180° = 1-(-1) = 2$ থেকে আসে।
Circular Loop (Centre) radius $a$
$$\bar{H} = \frac{I}{2a}\,\hat{a}_z \quad \text{A/m}$$
Loop-এর কেন্দ্রে। Axis বরাবর দূরত্ব $z$ হলে: $\bar{H} = \dfrac{Ia^2}{2(a^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\hat{a}_z$
Solenoid ($N$ turns, len $L$) Inside
$$\bar{H} = \frac{NI}{L}\,\hat{a}_z \quad \text{A/m}$$
1.2
Ampere's Circuital Law
Symmetry থাকলে সবচেয়ে দ্রুত $\bar{H}$ পাওয়ার পদ্ধতি
▾
Integral Form $\oint \bar{H}\cdot d\bar{l}$
$$\oint_L \bar{H}\cdot d\bar{l} = I_{enc} \quad \text{A}$$
Amperian loop বেছে নাও যেন $\bar{H}$ constant ও tangential থাকে।
Point / Differential Form Maxwell 4th (static)
$$\nabla\times\bar{H} = \bar{J}$$
Toroid ($N$ turns) radius $\rho$
$$\bar{H} = \frac{NI}{2\pi\rho}\,\hat{a}_\phi \quad \text{A/m (inside)}$$
Toroid-এর বাইরে $\bar{H} = 0$। $N$ = মোট পাকের সংখ্যা।
1.3
Magnetic Flux Density ও Vector Potential
$\bar{B}$, $\Psi$, এবং $\bar{A}$
▾
Flux Density $\bar{B}$ (Tesla)
$$\bar{B} = \mu\bar{H} = \mu_0\mu_r\bar{H} \quad \text{T}$$
$\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ H/m। Free space-এ $\mu_r = 1$।
Magnetic Flux $\Psi$ (Weber)
$$\Psi = \int_S \bar{B}\cdot d\bar{S} \quad \text{Wb}$$
Gauss's Law (Magnetic) No monopole
$$\oint_S \bar{B}\cdot d\bar{S} = 0 \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \nabla\cdot\bar{B} = 0$$
Vector Potential $\bar{A}$ (Wb/m)
$$\bar{B} = \nabla\times\bar{A}$$
$\bar{A}$ for Line Current
$$\bar{A} = \frac{\mu I}{4\pi}\oint\frac{d\bar{l}}{R} \quad \text{Wb/m}$$
Poisson's Eq. (Magnetic) $\nabla^2\bar{A}$
$$\nabla^2\bar{A} = -\mu\bar{J}$$
1.4
Magnetic Force ও Inductance
Lorentz Force, বল, এবং $L$
▾
Lorentz Force $\bar{F}$ (Newton)
$$\bar{F} = q(\bar{E} + \bar{u}\times\bar{B}) \quad \text{N}$$
$\bar{u}$ = চার্জ $q$-এর বেগ। Pure magnetic: $\bar{F} = q(\bar{u}\times\bar{B})$
Force on Current Element $d\bar{F}$
$$d\bar{F} = I\,d\bar{l}\times\bar{B} \quad \text{N}$$
Force between Two Wires per unit length
$$\frac{F}{L} = \frac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2}{2\pi d} \quad \text{N/m}$$
$d$ = দুই parallel তারের মধ্যে দূরত্ব। Same direction current → attractive।
Torque on Loop $\bar{T}$
$$\bar{T} = \bar{m}\times\bar{B} = NIA\hat{a}_n\times\bar{B} \quad \text{N·m}$$
$\bar{m} = NIA\hat{a}_n$ = magnetic dipole moment, $A$ = loop area
Inductance $L$ (Henry)
$$L = \frac{\lambda}{I} = \frac{N\Psi}{I} \quad \text{H}$$
$\lambda = N\Psi$ = flux linkage। Self-inductance হলে same coil-এর $\Psi$ ও $I$।
Mutual Inductance $M$
$$M = \frac{N_2\Psi_{12}}{I_1} = \frac{N_1\Psi_{21}}{I_2} \quad \text{H}$$
Energy in Magnetic Field $W_m$
$$W_m = \frac{1}{2}LI^2 = \frac{1}{2}\int_v \bar{B}\cdot\bar{H}\,dv \quad \text{J}$$
Magnetic Energy Density $w_m$ (J/m³)
$$w_m = \frac{1}{2}\mu|\bar{H}|^2 = \frac{|\bar{B}|^2}{2\mu} \quad \text{J/m}^3$$
1.5
Magnetostatic Boundary Conditions
দুই মিডিয়ামের সীমানায় $\bar{B}$ ও $\bar{H}$
▾
Normal $\bar{B}$ (লম্ব) Continuous
$$B_{n1} = B_{n2} \quad \Rightarrow \quad \mu_1 H_{n1} = \mu_2 H_{n2}$$
$\bar{B}$-এর লম্ব অংশ সীমানায় সর্বদা continuous থাকে।
Tangential $\bar{H}$ (স্পর্শক) Discontinuous
$$H_{t1} - H_{t2} = K \quad \text{(A/m, surface current density)}$$
Surface current $K = 0$ হলে: $H_{t1} = H_{t2}$
Refraction Law $\theta_1, \theta_2$
$$\frac{\tan\theta_1}{\tan\theta_2} = \frac{\mu_1}{\mu_2}$$
2.1
Maxwell's 4 Equations — একসাথে
Time-varying field-এর জন্য সম্পূর্ণ রূপ (উভয় form)
▾
সূত্র Differential Form Integral Form
Gauss's Lawfor Electricity Diff
$\nabla\cdot\bar{D} = \rho_v$		 Intg
$\displaystyle\oint_S \bar{D}\cdot d\bar{S} = Q_{enc}$
Gauss's Lawfor Magnetism Diff
$\nabla\cdot\bar{B} = 0$		 Intg
$\displaystyle\oint_S \bar{B}\cdot d\bar{S} = 0$
Faraday's LawInduction Diff
$\nabla\times\bar{E} = -\dfrac{\partial\bar{B}}{\partial t}$		 Intg
$\displaystyle\oint_L \bar{E}\cdot d\bar{l} = -\int_S\frac{\partial\bar{B}}{\partial t}\cdot d\bar{S}$
Ampere-MaxwellModified Ampere's Law Diff
$\nabla\times\bar{H} = \bar{J} + \dfrac{\partial\bar{D}}{\partial t}$		 Intg
$\displaystyle\oint_L \bar{H}\cdot d\bar{l} = \int_S\!\left(\bar{J}+\frac{\partial\bar{D}}{\partial t}\right)\!\cdot d\bar{S}$
Static vs Time-varying:  Static-এ $\partial/\partial t = 0$ রাখলে Maxwell's equations থেকেই Electrostatics ও Magnetostatics-এর সূত্র পাবে।
2.2
Faraday's Law — EMF Induction
তিনটি case আলাদা করে বোঝো
▾
General EMF $V_{emf}$ (Volt)
$$V_{emf} = -\frac{d\Psi}{dt} = -\frac{d}{dt}\int_S \bar{B}\cdot d\bar{S} \quad \text{V}$$
Negative sign = Lenz's Law — induced EMF opposes the change
Transformer EMF Stationary loop
$$V_{emf} = -\int_S\frac{\partial\bar{B}}{\partial t}\cdot d\bar{S} \quad \text{V}$$
Loop স্থির, $\bar{B}$ সময়ের সাথে পরিবর্তন হচ্ছে।
Motional EMF Moving loop
$$V_{emf} = \oint_L (\bar{v}\times\bar{B})\cdot d\bar{l} \quad \text{V}$$
$\bar{B}$ constant, loop বেগ $\bar{v}$-তে নড়ছে।
Total EMF (Combined)
$$V_{emf} = -\int_S\frac{\partial\bar{B}}{\partial t}\cdot d\bar{S} + \oint_L(\bar{v}\times\bar{B})\cdot d\bar{l}$$
2.3
Displacement Current
Maxwell-এর সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ অবদান
▾
Displacement Current Density $\bar{J}_d$ (A/m²)
$$\bar{J}_d = \frac{\partial\bar{D}}{\partial t} = \varepsilon\frac{\partial\bar{E}}{\partial t}$$
Capacitor gap-এ real current নেই, কিন্তু $\bar{J}_d$ আছে — এটাই continuity রক্ষা করে।
Conduction Current Density $\bar{J}_c$
$$\bar{J}_c = \sigma\bar{E} \quad \text{A/m}^2$$
Total Current Density $\bar{J}_{total}$
$$\bar{J} = \bar{J}_c + \bar{J}_d = \sigma\bar{E} + \varepsilon\frac{\partial\bar{E}}{\partial t}$$
Displacement Current $I_d$ (Ampere)
$$I_d = \int_S \bar{J}_d\cdot d\bar{S} = \varepsilon\frac{d}{dt}\int_S\bar{E}\cdot d\bar{S} \quad \text{A}$$
Ratio মনে রাখো:  $\dfrac{J_d}{J_c} = \dfrac{\omega\varepsilon}{\sigma} = \dfrac{1}{\tan\delta_c}$  — এটাই loss tangent-এর বিপরীত।
2.4
Maxwell's Equations — Phasor Form
Sinusoidal steady-state এ $\partial/\partial t \to j\omega$
▾
Faraday
$$\nabla\times\bar{E}_s = -j\omega\mu\bar{H}_s$$
Ampere-Maxwell
$$\nabla\times\bar{H}_s = (\sigma + j\omega\varepsilon)\bar{E}_s = \bar{J}_s + j\omega\varepsilon\bar{E}_s$$
Gauss (Electric)
$$\nabla\cdot\bar{D}_s = \rho_{vs}$$
Gauss (Magnetic)
$$\nabla\cdot\bar{B}_s = 0$$
Wave Equation:  Phasor Maxwell থেকে derive করা যায়:  $\nabla^2\bar{E}_s = \gamma^2\bar{E}_s$  যেখানে $\gamma^2 = j\omega\mu(\sigma + j\omega\varepsilon)$
3.1
Cut-off Frequency ও Mode — $TE_{mn}$ / $TM_{mn}$
Rectangular waveguide: width $a$, height $b$ (সাধারণত $a > b$)
▾
Cut-off Frequency $f_{c,mn}$
$$f_{c,mn} = \frac{u'}{2}\sqrt{\left(\frac{m}{a}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{n}{b}\right)^2} \quad \text{Hz}$$
$m, n$ = integer mode indices (0, 1, 2, …). $u' = c/\sqrt{\mu_r\varepsilon_r}$ = unbounded medium speed. Air/free space: $u' = c = 3\times10^8$ m/s
Dominant Mode ($TE_{10}$) $m{=}1, n{=}0$
$$f_{c,10} = \frac{u'}{2a} \quad \text{Hz}$$
সবচেয়ে কম cut-off frequency। Practical waveguide-এ এই mode-এ operate করা হয়।
Cut-off Wavenumber $k_c$
$$k_c = \frac{\omega_c}{u'} = \pi\sqrt{\left(\frac{m}{a}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{n}{b}\right)^2} \quad \text{rad/m}$$
Mode existence conditions
TE mode ($TM_{mn}$)
$m = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$
$n = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$
(m ও n একসাথে 0 নয়)
$E_z = 0$
TM mode ($TM_{mn}$)
$m = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$
$n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$
(m বা n = 0 allowed নয়)
$H_z = 0$
Operation condition:  Signal propagate করবে যদি $f > f_c$। $f < f_c$ হলে evanescent mode — signal attenuate হয়, propagate করে না।
3.2
Propagation Parameters
$\beta_g$, $v_p$, $v_g$, $\lambda_g$ — সবই $(f_c/f)$ দিয়ে চেনা যায়
▾
Phase Constant (Guided) $\beta_g$ (rad/m)
$$\beta_g = \beta'\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{f_c}{f}\right)^2} \quad \text{rad/m}$$
$\beta' = \omega/u' = \omega\sqrt{\mu\varepsilon}$ = unbounded medium-এর phase constant
Phase Velocity $u_p$ (m/s)
$$u_p = \frac{u'}{\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}} \geq u' \quad \text{m/s}$$
Phase velocity সবসময় $u'$ এর চেয়ে বড় — কিন্তু এটা signal speed নয়।
Group Velocity $u_g$ (m/s)
$$u_g = u'\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{f_c}{f}\right)^2} \leq u' \quad \text{m/s}$$
Signal ও energy এই বেগে travel করে। সর্বদা $u' = c$ এর কম।
Velocity Relation Important!
$$u_p \cdot u_g = (u')^2 \quad \text{(air: } = c^2\text{)}$$
Guided Wavelength $\lambda_g$ (m)
$$\lambda_g = \frac{\lambda'}{\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}} \quad \text{m}$$
$\lambda' = u'/f$ = unbounded medium wavelength। $\lambda_g > \lambda'$ সর্বদা।
Attenuation ($f < f_c$) Evanescent
$$\alpha = \beta'\sqrt{\left(\frac{f_c}{f}\right)^2 - 1} \quad \text{Np/m}$$
3.3
Wave Impedance — TE ও TM Mode
$\eta_{TE}$ ও $\eta_{TM}$ সবসময় real (propagating mode)
▾
TE Mode Impedance $\eta_{TE}$
$$\eta_{TE} = \frac{\eta'}{\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}} \geq \eta' \quad \Omega$$
TM Mode Impedance $\eta_{TM}$
$$\eta_{TM} = \eta'\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{f_c}{f}\right)^2} \leq \eta' \quad \Omega$$
$\eta' = \sqrt{\mu/\varepsilon}$ = medium intrinsic impedance। Free space: $\eta' = 120\pi \approx 377\,\Omega$
Impedance Product Relation
$$\eta_{TE}\cdot\eta_{TM} = (\eta')^2$$
Pattern মনে রাখো:  TE → "উপরে": $\eta_{TE} = \eta'/\sqrt{\cdots}$  ·  TM → "নিচে": $\eta_{TM} = \eta'\sqrt{\cdots}$
Phase velocity-ও TE-এর মতো ($u_p$), group velocity TM-এর মতো ($u_g$)।
3.4
$TE_{10}$ Mode — Field Components
Dominant mode, সবচেয়ে বেশি পরীক্ষায় আসে
▾
$H_z$ (dominant)
$$H_z = H_0\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right)e^{-j\beta_g z}$$
$E_y$
$$E_y = \frac{j\omega\mu a}{\pi}H_0\sin\!\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right)e^{-j\beta_g z}$$
$H_x$
$$H_x = \frac{j\beta_g a}{\pi}H_0\sin\!\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right)e^{-j\beta_g z}$$
$E_x = H_y = E_z = 0$
$TE_{10}$ mode-এ এই components শূন্য।
Relation:  $E_y / H_x = -\eta_{TE}$  ·  $\hat{a}_z \times \bar{E} = \eta_{TE}\bar{H}_t$

Waveguide Quick Reference — একনজরে
▾
Step-by-step — Waveguide ম্যাথ সলভ করো

Step 1 — Identify mode: $m, n$ দেওয়া থাকলে TE নাকি TM check করো। $TE_{10}$ → dominant, সবচেয়ে সহজ।

Step 2 — Cut-off frequency: $f_{c,mn} = \dfrac{u'}{2}\sqrt{(m/a)^2+(n/b)^2}$ দিয়ে $f_c$ বের করো।

Step 3 — Check propagation: দেওয়া $f > f_c$ কিনা দেখো। হলে propagating; না হলে evanescent।

Step 4 — Parameters: $\beta_g$, $u_p$, $u_g$, $\lambda_g$, $\eta_{TE/TM}$ — সবই $\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}$ দিয়ে বের করো।
