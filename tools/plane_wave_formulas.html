Complete Formula Reference — সকল সূত্র + Nomenclature

8 Categories Lossy · Lossless · Good Conductor Normal & Oblique Incidence
00
মিডিয়াম চেনার শর্ত — Classification of Media
Loss Tangent দেখে মিডিয়াম identify করো
▾
Lossless / Perfect Dielectric: $\sigma = 0$ Low-Loss / Good Dielectric: $\dfrac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon} \ll 1$ Good Conductor / High-Loss: $\dfrac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon} \gg 1$
Loss Tangent হলো মিডিয়ামের conduction current ও displacement current এর অনুপাত:  $\tan\delta_c = \dfrac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon}$  — এর মান দেখেই বলতে পারবে কোন case apply করতে হবে।
01
General / Lossy Dielectric Medium
সবচেয়ে general case — এখান থেকেই বাকি সব derive হয়
▾
Propagation Constant $\gamma$ (gamma)
$$\gamma = \alpha + j\beta = \sqrt{j\omega\mu(\sigma + j\omega\epsilon)} \quad \text{m}^{-1}$$
Attenuation Constant $\alpha$ (alpha)
$$\alpha = \omega\sqrt{\frac{\mu\epsilon}{2}\left[\sqrt{1+\left(\frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon}\right)^2} - 1\right]} \quad \text{Np/m}$$
Field amplitude প্রতি মিটারে $e^{-\alpha}$ গুণ কমে। Np = Neper.
Phase Constant $\beta$ (beta)
$$\beta = \omega\sqrt{\frac{\mu\epsilon}{2}\left[\sqrt{1+\left(\frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon}\right)^2} + 1\right]} \quad \text{rad/m}$$
Wave number নামেও পরিচিত। একটি wavelength-এ phase $2\pi$ rad পরিবর্তন হয়।
Intrinsic Impedance $\eta$ (eta)
$$\eta = \sqrt{\frac{j\omega\mu}{\sigma + j\omega\epsilon}} = |\eta|\angle\theta_\eta \quad \Omega$$
$\theta_\eta$ হলো $\bar{H}$-field কতটুকু $\bar{E}$-field এর পিছিয়ে আছে তার phase angle। Lossy medium-এ $\theta_\eta > 0$।
Phase Velocity $v_p$ বা $u_p$
$$v_p = \frac{\omega}{\beta} \quad \text{m/s}$$
Group Velocity $v_g$
$$v_g = \frac{d\omega}{d\beta} \quad \text{m/s}$$
Signal বা energy এই বেগে travel করে। Dispersionless medium-এ $v_g = v_p$।
Wavelength $\lambda$ (lambda)
$$\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{\beta} \quad \text{m}$$
Skin Depth $\delta$ (delta)
$$\delta = \frac{1}{\alpha} \quad \text{m}$$
যতটুকু ভেতরে গেলে field amplitude $e^{-1} \approx 36.8\%$ এ নামে।
Loss Tangent $\tan\delta_c$
$$\tan\delta_c = \frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon}$$
02
Lossless Dielectric
$\sigma = 0$ হলে সহজ হয়ে যায়
▾
Attenuation $\alpha$
$$\alpha = 0$$
Phase Constant $\beta$
$$\beta = \omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$$
Intrinsic Impedance $\eta$
$$\eta = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{\epsilon}} = \frac{120\pi\sqrt{\mu_r}}{\sqrt{\epsilon_r}} \angle 0^\circ \quad \Omega$$
Real — $\bar{E}$ ও $\bar{H}$ in-phase থাকে।
Phase Velocity $u_p$
$$u_p = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}} = \frac{c}{\sqrt{\mu_r\epsilon_r}}$$
03
Good Conductor
$\sigma \gg \omega\epsilon$ — metal-এর মতো
▾
$\alpha = \beta$ (equal!) $\alpha, \beta$
$$\alpha = \beta = \sqrt{\pi f \mu \sigma} \quad \text{Np/m, rad/m}$$
Good conductor-এ attenuation ও phase constant সমান হয়।
Intrinsic Impedance $\eta$
$$\eta = \sqrt{\frac{\omega\mu}{\sigma}}\angle 45^\circ = \frac{1+j}{\sigma\delta} \quad \Omega$$
$\bar{H}$ সবসময় $\bar{E}$ থেকে $45^\circ$ পিছিয়ে থাকে।
Skin Depth $\delta$
$$\delta = \frac{1}{\alpha} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi f \mu \sigma}} \quad \text{m}$$
Surface Resistance $R_s$
$$R_s = \frac{1}{\sigma\delta} = \sqrt{\frac{\pi f\mu}{\sigma}} \quad \Omega/\square$$
Conductor surface-এ unit area per square resistance।
04
Electric ও Magnetic Field — $\bar{E}$ এবং $\bar{H}$
Time-domain expression, $+z$ direction propagation ধরে
▾
Electric Field $\bar{E}(z,t)$
$$\bar{E}(z,t) = E_0\, e^{-\alpha z}\cos(\omega t - \beta z)\,\hat{a}_x \quad \text{V/m}$$
$E_0$ = peak amplitude, $e^{-\alpha z}$ = exponential decay envelope, $(\omega t - \beta z)$ = traveling phase
Magnetic Field $\bar{H}(z,t)$
$$\bar{H}(z,t) = \frac{E_0}{|\eta|}\, e^{-\alpha z}\cos(\omega t - \beta z - \theta_\eta)\,\hat{a}_y \quad \text{A/m}$$
$|\eta|$ = intrinsic impedance magnitude, $\theta_\eta$ = impedance phase angle
shortcut — $\bar{H}$ বের করার সহজ পথ
$\bar{H}$ from $\bar{E}$ Cross Product Rule
$$\bar{H} = \frac{1}{\eta}(\hat{a}_k \times \bar{E})$$
$\hat{a}_k$ = unit vector in propagation direction। এটা সবচেয়ে দ্রুত $\bar{H}$ পাওয়ার পদ্ধতি।
phasor / complex domain
Phasor Form $\bar{E}_s(z)$
$$\bar{E}_s(z) = E_0\, e^{-\gamma z}\,\hat{a}_x = E_0\,e^{-(\alpha+j\beta)z}\,\hat{a}_x \quad \text{V/m}$$
Time-domain recover করতে: $\text{Re}\{\bar{E}_s\, e^{j\omega t}\}$
05
Power Density — Poynting Vector
EM wave কতটুকু power বহন করছে
▾
Poynting Vector $\bar{\mathcal{P}}$
$$\bar{\mathcal{P}} = \bar{E} \times \bar{H} \quad \text{W/m}^2$$
Instantaneous power density — direction = propagation direction
Time-Avg Power (Lossy) $\bar{P}_{avg}$
$$\bar{P}_{avg} = \frac{E_0^2}{2|\eta|}\,e^{-2\alpha z}\cos\theta_\eta\;\hat{a}_z \quad \text{W/m}^2$$
Time-Avg Power (Lossless) $\bar{P}_{avg}$
$$\bar{P}_{avg} = \frac{E_0^2}{2\eta}\;\hat{a}_z \quad \text{W/m}^2 \quad (\alpha=0,\;\theta_\eta=0)$$
Complex Poynting $\bar{P}_c$
$$\bar{P}_c = \frac{1}{2}\bar{E}_s \times \bar{H}_s^*$$
$\bar{H}_s^*$ = complex conjugate of $\bar{H}$ phasor। $\bar{P}_{avg} = \text{Re}\{\bar{P}_c\}$
Total Power $P_{total}$
$$P_{total} = \iint_S \bar{P}_{avg} \cdot d\bar{S} \quad \text{W}$$
06
Normal Incidence — Reflection & Transmission
Wave লম্বভাবে boundary-তে পড়লে
▾
Reflection Coefficient $\Gamma$ (Gamma)
$$\Gamma = \frac{\eta_2 - \eta_1}{\eta_2 + \eta_1} = \frac{E_{r0}}{E_{i0}}$$
$\eta_1$ = incident medium, $\eta_2$ = transmitted medium। $-1 \le \Gamma \le 1$ (real case)
Transmission Coefficient $\tau$ (tau)
$$\tau = \frac{2\eta_2}{\eta_2 + \eta_1} = \frac{E_{t0}}{E_{i0}}$$
Key Relation $\Gamma, \tau$
$$1 + \Gamma = \tau$$
SWR / VSWR $s$
$$s = \frac{1 + |\Gamma|}{1 - |\Gamma|}$$
Standing Wave Ratio। $s = 1$ → no reflection; $s = \infty$ → total reflection
Power ratios
Reflected Power %
$$|\Gamma|^2 \times 100\%$$
Transmitted Power %
$$(1 - |\Gamma|^2) \times 100\%$$
Standing wave field values
Max Field Amplitude $|E_{max}|$
$$|E_{max}| = |E_{i0}|\,(1 + |\Gamma|)$$
Min Field Amplitude $|E_{min}|$
$$|E_{min}| = |E_{i0}|\,(1 - |\Gamma|)$$
07
Oblique Incidence — কোণে আপতিত
Snell's Law, Brewster Angle, Critical Angle
▾
Snell's Law $\theta_i, \theta_t$
$$\frac{\sin\theta_i}{\sin\theta_t} = \frac{\beta_2}{\beta_1} = \frac{v_{p1}}{v_{p2}} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu_2\epsilon_2}{\mu_1\epsilon_1}}$$
$\theta_i$ = incident angle, $\theta_t$ = transmitted (refracted) angle — surface normal থেকে পরিমাপ করা হয়
Brewster Angle $\theta_B$
$$\tan\theta_B = \sqrt{\frac{\epsilon_2}{\epsilon_1}} \quad \text{(parallel/TM polarization)}$$
এই কোণে $\Gamma_\parallel = 0$ — কোনো reflected power থাকে না। শুধু TM (parallel) polarization-এ exists করে।
Critical Angle $\theta_c$
$$\sin\theta_c = \sqrt{\frac{\epsilon_2}{\epsilon_1}} = \frac{n_2}{n_1} \quad (\epsilon_2 < \epsilon_1)$$
ঘন থেকে হালকা মাধ্যমে ($\epsilon_1 > \epsilon_2$) যাওয়ার সময় প্রযোজ্য। $\theta_i > \theta_c$ হলে Total Internal Reflection ঘটে।
Fresnel Coefficients (Perpendicular / TE polarization)
$\Gamma_\perp$ TE / s-pol
$$\Gamma_\perp = \frac{\eta_2\cos\theta_i - \eta_1\cos\theta_t}{\eta_2\cos\theta_i + \eta_1\cos\theta_t}$$
$\tau_\perp$ TE / s-pol
$$\tau_\perp = \frac{2\eta_2\cos\theta_i}{\eta_2\cos\theta_i + \eta_1\cos\theta_t}$$
Fresnel Coefficients (Parallel / TM polarization)
$\Gamma_\parallel$ TM / p-pol
$$\Gamma_\parallel = \frac{\eta_2\cos\theta_t - \eta_1\cos\theta_i}{\eta_2\cos\theta_t + \eta_1\cos\theta_i}$$
$\tau_\parallel$ TM / p-pol
$$\tau_\parallel = \frac{2\eta_2\cos\theta_i}{\eta_2\cos\theta_t + \eta_1\cos\theta_i}$$

Physical Constants — মুখস্থ রাখো
Free space / vacuum values
▾
$\epsilon_0$ $8.854 \times 10^{-12}$ F/m
$\mu_0$ $4\pi \times 10^{-7}$ H/m
$c$ $3 \times 10^{8}$ m/s
$\eta_0$ $120\pi \approx 377$ Ω
$c = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}}$  ·  $\eta_0 = \mu_0 c = \sqrt{\dfrac{\mu_0}{\epsilon_0}}$  ·  $\mu_r = \epsilon_r = 1$ (free space)
