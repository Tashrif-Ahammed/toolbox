Q 01
Math · Rectangular Waveguide
β, Phase Velocity, Group Velocity ও Guided Wavelength নির্ণয়
▼

A rectangular waveguide (2.5 cm × 1.5 cm) operates at 7.5 GHz. Calculate (i) phase constant $\beta$, (ii) phase velocity $u_p$, (iii) group velocity $u_g$, (iv) guided wavelength $\lambda_g$ — for hollow and dielectric-filled ($\epsilon_r = 2.1$) cases. Also find cutoff frequencies of all propagating modes for $a = 2.286$ cm, $b = 1.016$ cm at 10 GHz.

Key Formulae — Rectangular Waveguide (TE/TM mode)

$$f_{c,mn} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}}\sqrt{\left(\frac{m}{a}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{n}{b}\right)^2}$$
$$\beta = \sqrt{\left(\frac{\omega}{u}\right)^2 - \left(\frac{m\pi}{a}\right)^2 - \left(\frac{n\pi}{b}\right)^2} = \frac{\omega}{u}\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{f_c}{f}\right)^2}$$

Part A — 2.5 cm × 1.5 cm at 7.5 GHz (Dominant Mode TE₁₀)

Cutoff Frequency (TE₁₀)
$$f_c = \frac{c}{2a} = \frac{3\times10^8}{2\times 0.025} = 6\times10^9 = 6 \text{ GHz}$$
Hollow Guide (air: u = c = 3×10⁸ m/s)
$$\beta = \frac{\omega}{c}\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2} = \frac{2\pi\times7.5\times10^9}{3\times10^8}\sqrt{1-(6/7.5)^2}$$
$$= 50\pi\sqrt{1-0.64} = 50\pi\times 0.6 = 30\pi \approx \mathbf{94.25 \text{ rad/m}}$$
$$u_p = \frac{\omega}{\beta} = \frac{2\pi\times7.5\times10^9}{30\pi} = \frac{c}{\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}} = \frac{3\times10^8}{0.6} = \mathbf{5\times10^8 \text{ m/s}}$$
$$u_g = u_p\times\left(1-(f_c/f)^2\right) = c\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2} = 3\times10^8\times 0.6 = \mathbf{1.8\times10^8 \text{ m/s}}$$
$$\lambda_g = \frac{2\pi}{\beta} = \frac{2\pi}{30\pi} = \frac{1}{15} \approx \mathbf{0.0667 \text{ m} = 6.67 \text{ cm}}$$
⚡ Check: $u_p \times u_g = c^2$ — সবসময় verify করবে।
$5\times10^8 \times 1.8\times10^8 = 9\times10^{16} = (3\times10^8)^2$ ✓
Dielectric-filled (ε_r = 2.1, μ_r = 1)
$$u = \frac{c}{\sqrt{\epsilon_r}} = \frac{3\times10^8}{\sqrt{2.1}} = 2.07\times10^8 \text{ m/s}$$
$$f_c^{(\text{diel})} = \frac{u}{2a} = \frac{2.07\times10^8}{2\times0.025} = 4.14 \text{ GHz}$$
$$\beta_d = \frac{2\pi f}{u}\sqrt{1-(f_c^d/f)^2} = \frac{2\pi\times7.5\times10^9}{2.07\times10^8}\sqrt{1-(4.14/7.5)^2}$$
$$= 227.7\sqrt{1-0.305} = 227.7\times 0.834 \approx \mathbf{189.9 \text{ rad/m}}$$

Part B — Cutoff Frequencies (a=2.286 cm, b=1.016 cm, f=10 GHz)

Air-filled: $c = 3\times10^8$ m/s। TE_{mn} ও TM_{mn} modes (m≥0,n≥0, not both zero; TM: m≥1,n≥1):

$$f_{c,mn} = \frac{c}{2}\sqrt{\left(\frac{m}{a}\right)^2+\left(\frac{n}{b}\right)^2}$$
Mode$f_c$ (GHz)Propagates at 10 GHz?
TE₁₀$\dfrac{3\times10^8}{2\times0.02286} = 6.56$✓ Yes
TE₂₀$2\times6.56 = 13.12$✗ No
TE₀₁$\dfrac{3\times10^8}{2\times0.01016} = 14.76$✗ No
TE₁₁, TM₁₁$\dfrac{c}{2}\sqrt{(1/a)^2+(1/b)^2} = 16.16$✗ No
Conclusion

এই waveguide-এ 10 GHz-এ শুধুমাত্র TE₁₀ mode propagate করে — single-mode operation।

Q 02
Theory · Waveguide Fundamentals
TEM Mode অসম্ভব কেন + Waveguide একটি High-Pass Filter
▼

"The TEM mode does not exist in a waveguide" — justify with mathematical explanation. Explain how a waveguide acts like a high-pass filter.

Part A — TEM Mode Waveguide-এ নেই কেন

TEM Mode: Transverse Electromagnetic Mode — যেখানে $E_z = 0$ এবং $H_z = 0$ (কোনো longitudinal field component নেই)।

1

TEM-এ $E_z = H_z = 0$। Maxwell থেকে transverse field equations:

$$E_x = \frac{-j\omega\mu}{k_c^2}\frac{\partial H_z}{\partial y} + \frac{-j\beta}{k_c^2}\frac{\partial E_z}{\partial x}$$

TEM হলে উভয় শর্ত শূন্য, তাই: $E_x = E_y = H_x = H_y = 0$ যদি না $k_c = 0$।

2

$k_c = 0$ মানে cutoff wavenumber শূন্য, অর্থাৎ:

$$k_c^2 = \left(\frac{m\pi}{a}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{n\pi}{b}\right)^2 = 0 \implies m = n = 0$$

কিন্তু $m = n = 0$ হলে কোনো transverse field variation নেই — এটি trivial (null) solution।

3

Gauss's law: $\nabla_t^2 E_z = 0$ in the guide। TEM-এ $E_z = 0$ সর্বত্র → $E$ transverse-এ যদি কোনো boundary থেকে আসে, সেটা হতে পারে শুধু conducting inner conductor থাকলে (coaxial cable-এর মতো)।

Single-conductor hollow waveguide-এ কোনো inner conductor নেই, তাই Laplace's equation-এ $V = 0$ at wall → interior-এ $V = 0$ সর্বত্র → $E_t = 0$ → no TEM।

Conclusion

Single-conductor hollow rectangular waveguide-এ TEM mode exist করে না। TEM-এর জন্য অন্তত দুটি conductor লাগে (coaxial cable, twin-wire line)।

Part B — Waveguide as High-Pass Filter

Cutoff frequency $f_c$-এর নিচে: $f < f_c$ হলে $\beta$ imaginary হয় → wave propagate করে না, বরং exponentially decay করে (evanescent mode)।

$$\beta = \frac{\omega}{c}\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}$$

যখন $f < f_c$: $1-(f_c/f)^2 < 0$ → $\beta$ purely imaginary → $e^{-j\beta z} = e^{-\alpha z}$ (attenuation)।

যখন $f > f_c$: $\beta$ real → wave propagates freely।

High-Pass Analogy

Filter analogy: শুধু $f > f_c$ সংকেত pass করে, নিচেরগুলো block করে — ঠিক যেমন high-pass filter।

$$\text{Stop band: } f < f_c \quad\quad \text{Pass band: } f > f_c$$
📡 Evanescent mode: $f < f_c$ হলে field exponentially decays হয় কিন্তু power propagate করে না। Phase shift নেই, শুধু attenuation।

Q 03
Derivation · Time-varying Fields
Ampere's Law → Maxwell's Curl Equation (স্ট্যাটিক ও টাইম-ভ্যারিং)
▼

Using Ampere's circuital law, obtain Maxwell's curl equation for magnetostatic fields. State with necessary expression how this curl equation differs in time-varying fields.

Ampere's Circuital Law (Static)

বিবৃতি: যেকোনো closed path-এ $\bar{H}$-এর line integral ঐ path দ্বারা আবদ্ধ মোট current-এর সমান।

$$\oint_L \bar{H}\cdot d\bar{l} = I_{enc} = \int_S \bar{J}\cdot d\bar{S}$$
1

Stokes' theorem প্রয়োগ: $\oint_L\bar{H}\cdot d\bar{l} = \int_S(\nabla\times\bar{H})\cdot d\bar{S}$

2

তুলনা: $\int_S(\nabla\times\bar{H})\cdot d\bar{S} = \int_S\bar{J}\cdot d\bar{S}$ — যেকোনো surface-এ:

$$\boxed{\nabla\times\bar{H} = \bar{J}} \quad \text{(Magnetostatic)}$$

Inconsistency in Time-Varying Fields

3

$\nabla\times\bar{H} = \bar{J}$-এর divergence: $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\bar{H}) = \nabla\cdot\bar{J}$।

কিন্তু vector identity অনুযায়ী: $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\bar{H}) = 0$ সর্বদা।

সুতরাং: $\nabla\cdot\bar{J} = 0$ — কিন্তু continuity equation থেকে $\nabla\cdot\bar{J} = -\partial\rho_v/\partial t$।

Time-varying field-এ $\partial\rho_v/\partial t \neq 0$ → contradiction! Static equation ভাঙে।

Maxwell's Correction — Displacement Current

4

Maxwell একটি নতুন term $\bar{J}_d$ (displacement current density) যোগ করলেন:

$$\bar{J}_d = \frac{\partial\bar{D}}{\partial t} = \epsilon\frac{\partial\bar{E}}{\partial t}$$
5

সংশোধিত Ampere's Law (Maxwell's 4th Equation):

$$\boxed{\nabla\times\bar{H} = \bar{J} + \frac{\partial\bar{D}}{\partial t}} \quad \text{(Time-varying)}$$
পার্থক্য
StaticTime-varying
$\nabla\times\bar{H} = \bar{J}$$\nabla\times\bar{H} = \bar{J} + \dfrac{\partial\bar{D}}{\partial t}$

Displacement current $\partial\bar{D}/\partial t$ যোগ হওয়ায় EM wave propagation সম্ভব হয় — এটিই Maxwell-এর সবচেয়ে বড় অবদান।

Q 04
Theory · Current Types
Conduction, Convection ও Displacement Current — সংজ্ঞা ও পার্থক্য
▼

Define conduction, convection and displacement currents. Explain the physical significance of displacement current density.

Conduction Current

সংজ্ঞা: Conductor-এ electric field-এর প্রভাবে free electron-এর drift motion থেকে যে current তৈরি হয়, তাকে conduction current বলে।

$$\bar{J}_{cond} = \sigma\bar{E} \quad \text{(A/m}^2\text{)}$$

Ohm's law-এর point form। Medium: conductor (σ ≠ 0)।

Convection Current

সংজ্ঞা: Volume charge density $\rho_v$ সহ কোনো medium যদি velocity $\bar{v}$-তে move করে, তাহলে যে current তৈরি হয়, তাকে convection current বলে।

$$\bar{J}_{conv} = \rho_v\bar{v} \quad \text{(A/m}^2\text{)}$$

Ohm's law follow করে না। Vacuum-এ electron beam, cathode ray tube-এ এই current।

Displacement Current

সংজ্ঞা: Time-varying electric field-এর ফলে capacitor-এর dielectric-এ যে equivalent current তৈরি হয়, তাকে displacement current বলে।

$$\bar{J}_d = \frac{\partial\bar{D}}{\partial t} = \epsilon\frac{\partial\bar{E}}{\partial t} \quad \text{(A/m}^2\text{)}$$

Physical Significance of Displacement Current

1

Capacitor-এ: AC circuit-এ capacitor-এর gap দিয়ে actual charge flow হয় না, কিন্তু $\partial\bar{D}/\partial t \neq 0$ → displacement current circuit complete করে।

2

EM Wave: Vacuum-তে কোনো conductor নেই, তবুও EM wave propagate করে। Time-varying E field → displacement current → H field → পুনরায় E field — এই চক্রই EM wave।

Q 05
Math · Ampere's Law
Hollow Conducting Cylinder — H field সর্বত্র নির্ণয়
▼

A hollow conducting cylinder has inner radius $a$ and outer radius $b$ and carries current $I$ uniformly along $+z$. Find magnetic field intensity $\bar{H}$ everywhere (i) $\rho < a$, (ii) $a < \rho < b$, (iii) $\rho > b$.

Ampere's Law Setup

Symmetry থেকে: $\bar{H} = H_\phi\hat{a}_\phi$, শুধু $\rho$-এর function।

$$\oint_L\bar{H}\cdot d\bar{l} = H_\phi\cdot 2\pi\rho = I_{enc}$$

Volume current density: $J = \dfrac{I}{\pi(b^2-a^2)}$ (A/m²) uniformly distributed।

Region 1: ρ < a (Hollow interior)

কোনো current নেই conductor-এর hollow অংশে:

$$I_{enc} = 0 \implies \bar{H} = 0$$
Region 2: a < ρ < b (Inside conductor)
$$I_{enc} = J\cdot\pi(\rho^2 - a^2) = \frac{I(\rho^2 - a^2)}{b^2 - a^2}$$
$$H_\phi\cdot 2\pi\rho = \frac{I(\rho^2-a^2)}{b^2-a^2}$$
$$\boxed{\bar{H} = \frac{I(\rho^2-a^2)}{2\pi\rho(b^2-a^2)}\hat{a}_\phi} \quad (a < \rho < b)$$
Region 3: ρ > b (Outside conductor)
$$I_{enc} = I \text{ (total current)}$$
$$\boxed{\bar{H} = \frac{I}{2\pi\rho}\hat{a}_\phi} \quad (\rho > b)$$
Summary
ρ < a
H = 0
a < ρ < b
H = I(ρ²−a²) / 2πρ(b²−a²)
ρ > b
H = I / 2πρ
📌 $\rho = a$-তে check: H = 0 (Region 1 boundary)। $\rho = b$-তে: Region 2 → $H = I/2\pi b$ = Region 3 ✓

Q 06
Theory & Derivation · Vector Potential
Magnetic Vector Potential $\bar{A}$ — সংজ্ঞা ও Biot-Savart থেকে Derivation
▼

Define vector magnetic potential $\bar{A}$. Show that $\bar{B} = \nabla\times\bar{A}$. Derive the equation of magnetic vector potential from Biot-Savart's law.

Magnetic Vector Potential — সংজ্ঞা

সংজ্ঞা: যে vector field $\bar{A}$-এর curl হলো magnetic flux density $\bar{B}$, তাকে magnetic vector potential বলে।

$$\boxed{\bar{B} = \nabla\times\bar{A}}$$

কেন $\bar{B} = \nabla\times\bar{A}$ ব্যবহার করা যায়?

1

Maxwell's equation: $\nabla\cdot\bar{B} = 0$ সর্বদা সত্য।

Vector identity: $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\bar{A}) = 0$ সর্বদা সত্য।

সুতরাং $\bar{B} = \nabla\times\bar{A}$ রাখলে $\nabla\cdot\bar{B} = 0$ automatically satisfied হয়।

Derivation from Biot-Savart's Law

2

Biot-Savart's Law:

$$d\bar{B} = \frac{\mu_0 I\,d\bar{l}\times\hat{a}_R}{4\pi R^2}$$
3

$\hat{a}_R/R^2 = -\nabla(1/R)$ identity ব্যবহার করলে:

$$d\bar{B} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi}\,d\bar{l}\times\left(-\nabla\frac{1}{R}\right) = \nabla\times\frac{\mu_0 I\,d\bar{l}}{4\pi R}$$
4

সমস্ত current path integrate করলে:

$$\bar{B} = \nabla\times\underbrace{\frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi}\oint\frac{d\bar{l}}{R}}_{\bar{A}}$$
$$\boxed{\bar{A} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi}\oint\frac{d\bar{l}}{R}} \quad \text{(line current)}$$

Volume Current Distribution

$$\bar{A} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\int_V\frac{\bar{J}\,dV'}{R}$$
Key Points

$\bar{A}$-এর direction = current-এর direction। $\bar{A}$ scalar potential V-এর মতো কিন্তু vector। Gauge condition: $\nabla\cdot\bar{A} = 0$ (Coulomb gauge)।

Q 07
Law & Derivation
Faraday's Law — $\nabla\times\bar{E} = -\dfrac{\partial\bar{B}}{\partial t}$ প্রমাণ
▼

State and explain Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction. Hence show that $\nabla\times\bar{E} = -\dfrac{\partial\bar{B}}{\partial t}$.

Faraday's Law — বিবৃতি

বিবৃতি: কোনো closed loop-এ induced emf, সেই loop-কে ভেদকারী magnetic flux-এর পরিবর্তনের হারের ঋণাত্মক মানের সমান।

$$\text{emf} = -\frac{d\Phi}{dt} = -\frac{d}{dt}\int_S\bar{B}\cdot d\bar{S}$$

Derivation: Differential Form

1

EMF in a closed loop L:

$$\text{emf} = \oint_L\bar{E}\cdot d\bar{l}$$
2

Faraday's law-এ রাখলে (stationary loop):

$$\oint_L\bar{E}\cdot d\bar{l} = -\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\int_S\bar{B}\cdot d\bar{S}$$
3

Stokes' Theorem: $\oint_L\bar{E}\cdot d\bar{l} = \int_S(\nabla\times\bar{E})\cdot d\bar{S}$

$$\int_S(\nabla\times\bar{E})\cdot d\bar{S} = -\int_S\frac{\partial\bar{B}}{\partial t}\cdot d\bar{S}$$
4

যেকোনো surface-এ সত্য হওয়ায়:

$$\boxed{\nabla\times\bar{E} = -\frac{\partial\bar{B}}{\partial t}}$$

এটিই Maxwell's 3rd Equation (Faraday's Law — differential form)।

Complete Set of Maxwell's Equations

Q 08
Theory · Maxwell's 2nd Equation
Magnetic Monopole-এর অনস্তিত্ব ও $\nabla\cdot\bar{B} = 0$ এর Physical Significance
▼

Explain Ampere's circuital law and the non-existence of magnetic monopole. What is the physical significance of $\nabla\cdot\bar{B} = 0$?

Magnetic Monopole

Magnetic Monopole: কাল্পনিক particle যার শুধু North বা শুধু South pole থাকবে — electric charge-এর মতো isolated magnetic charge। কিন্তু প্রকৃতিতে এটি পাওয়া যায় না।

∇·B = 0 Derivation

1

Biot-Savart law থেকে $\bar{B}$-এর divergence নিলে:

$$\nabla\cdot\bar{B} = \nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\bar{A}) = 0 \quad \text{(vector identity)}$$
2

Gauss's Law analog: $\oint_S\bar{B}\cdot d\bar{S} = 0$ — যেকোনো closed surface থেকে মোট magnetic flux শূন্য।

$$\boxed{\nabla\cdot\bar{B} = 0}$$

Physical Significance

১. Magnetic field lines সর্বদা closed loop — কোনো শুরু বা শেষ নেই।
২. যতটুকু flux কোনো closed surface-এ ঢোকে, ঠিক ততটুকু বের হয়।
৩. Isolated magnetic charge (monopole) exist করে না।
৪. Electric field ($\nabla\cdot\bar{E} = \rho_v/\epsilon_0$) এর সাথে asymmetry — charge আছে কিন্তু magnetic charge নেই।

🔮 Quantum theory-তে Dirac monopole predict করেছেন, কিন্তু এখনও experimentally detect হয়নি।

State how a waveguide differs from free space in the case of plane wave propagating through it. What is an evanescent mode?

Waveguide vs Free Space — তুলনা

Evanescent Mode

সংজ্ঞা: যখন operating frequency $f < f_c$ (cutoff frequency-র নিচে), তখন propagation constant purely imaginary হয় এবং field exponentially decay করে — energy propagate হয় না। এই mode-কে evanescent mode বলে।

$$f < f_c \implies \beta = j\alpha \implies e^{-j\beta z} = e^{-\alpha z} \text{ (decay only)}$$
$$\alpha = \frac{\omega}{c}\sqrt{(f_c/f)^2 - 1} \text{ Np/m}$$
Key Insight

Evanescent mode-এ field আছে কিন্তু power flow নেই। Short section-এ এটি impedance matching-এ ব্যবহার হয়। Energy "tunneling" effect-এর এটি একটি উদাহরণ।

Q 10
Math · Time-Varying Fields
H থেকে Maxwell ব্যবহার করে B, D, E ও β নির্ণয়
▼

A medium has characteristics $\mu, \epsilon, \sigma=0$. If $\bar{H} = 2\cos(10^{10}t - \beta x)\hat{a}_z$ A/m, use Maxwell's equations to find $\bar{B}$, $\bar{D}$, $\bar{E}$ and $\beta$.

Step 1 — B থেকে H

$$\bar{B} = \mu\bar{H} = 2\mu\cos(10^{10}t - \beta x)\hat{a}_z \text{ T}$$

Step 2 — E from Faraday's Law

$\nabla\times\bar{E} = -\partial\bar{B}/\partial t$ ব্যবহার করি।

$\bar{H}$ propagates in $+x$ direction with $\hat{a}_z$ component

Maxwell 4th equation ($\sigma=0$): $\nabla\times\bar{H} = \epsilon\dfrac{\partial\bar{E}}{\partial t}$

$$\nabla\times\bar{H} = \frac{\partial H_z}{\partial x}\hat{a}_y - \frac{\partial H_z}{\partial y}\hat{a}_x$$
$$= \frac{\partial}{\partial x}[2\cos(10^{10}t-\beta x)]\hat{a}_y = 2\beta\sin(10^{10}t-\beta x)\hat{a}_y$$
1

$\nabla\times\bar{H} = \epsilon\partial\bar{E}/\partial t$ থেকে:

$$\epsilon\frac{\partial\bar{E}}{\partial t} = 2\beta\sin(10^{10}t-\beta x)\hat{a}_y$$
$$\bar{E} = \int\frac{2\beta}{\epsilon}\sin(10^{10}t-\beta x)dt = \frac{-2\beta}{\epsilon\times 10^{10}}\cos(10^{10}t-\beta x)\hat{a}_y$$
$$\bar{E} = -\frac{2\beta}{\epsilon\times 10^{10}}\cos(10^{10}t-\beta x)\hat{a}_y \text{ V/m}$$

Step 3 — β নির্ণয় (Faraday's Law verify)

2

$\nabla\times\bar{E} = -\partial\bar{B}/\partial t$:

$$\frac{\partial E_y}{\partial x}\hat{a}_z = -\mu\frac{\partial H_z}{\partial t}\hat{a}_z$$
$$\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(-\frac{2\beta}{\epsilon\omega}\cos\phi\right) = -\frac{2\beta^2}{\epsilon\omega}\sin\phi$$
$$-\mu\frac{\partial H_z}{\partial t} = -\mu\times(-2\times10^{10}\sin\phi) = 2\mu\omega\sin\phi$$
3

Equating coefficients:

$$\frac{\beta^2}{\epsilon\omega} = \mu\omega \implies \beta^2 = \mu\epsilon\omega^2$$
$$\boxed{\beta = \omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon} = 10^{10}\sqrt{\mu\epsilon} \text{ rad/m}}$$

Step 4 — D

$$\bar{D} = \epsilon\bar{E} = -\frac{2\beta}{10^{10}}\cos(10^{10}t-\beta x)\hat{a}_y \text{ C/m}^2$$

Q 11
Theory · Boundary Conditions
Magnetostatic Boundary Conditions — দুটি Medium-এর Interface
▼

Explain the boundary conditions for electrostatic and magnetostatic fields at the interface of two different media.

Magnetostatic Boundary Conditions

Normal Component of B — Continuous

Gauss's law for magnetism: $\nabla\cdot\bar{B} = 0$

Pillbox Gaussian surface প্রয়োগ:

$$\oint_S\bar{B}\cdot d\bar{S} = 0 \implies B_{1n} = B_{2n}$$

অর্থাৎ: $\mu_1 H_{1n} = \mu_2 H_{2n}$

Tangential Component of H — Discontinuous

Ampere's law: $\oint_L\bar{H}\cdot d\bar{l} = I_{enc}$

Rectangular path (width → 0):

$$H_{1t} - H_{2t} = K_s \quad \text{(surface current density A/m)}$$

যদি কোনো surface current না থাকে ($K_s = 0$): $H_{1t} = H_{2t}$

Electrostatic Boundary Conditions (Comparison)

FieldNormal ComponentTangential Component
Electric $D_{1n} - D_{2n} = \rho_s$ (discontinuous) $E_{1t} = E_{2t}$ (continuous)
Magnetic $B_{1n} = B_{2n}$ (continuous) $H_{1t} - H_{2t} = K_s$ (discontinuous)
Memory Aid

Electric: Normal D jumps (charge), Tangential E continuous।
Magnetic: Normal B continuous, Tangential H jumps (surface current)।
মনে রাখো: "D jumps, B continues" — charge causes E jump, current causes H jump।

Q 12
Math · Biot-Savart Law
Circular Current Loop-এর Axis-এ H Field নির্ণয়
▼

Find the magnetic field intensity $\bar{H}$ on the axis of a circular current loop of radius $a$ carrying current $I$.

Setup

Loop: radius $a$, $xy$-plane-এ, center origin। Axis: $z$-axis। P at $(0,0,h)$।

1

Biot-Savart Law: $d\bar{H} = \dfrac{I\,d\bar{l}\times\hat{a}_R}{4\pi R^2}$

$d\bar{l} = a\,d\phi(-\sin\phi\hat{a}_x + \cos\phi\hat{a}_y)$

$\bar{R} = -a\cos\phi\hat{a}_x - a\sin\phi\hat{a}_y + h\hat{a}_z$

$R = \sqrt{a^2 + h^2}$

2

$d\bar{l}\times\hat{a}_R$ expand করলে। Symmetry দিয়ে $x$ ও $y$ components cancel হয়।

শুধু $z$-component টিকে থাকে:

$$(d\bar{l}\times\hat{a}_R)_z = \frac{a\,d\phi\cdot a}{R} = \frac{a^2\,d\phi}{R}$$
3

Integrate around loop ($0$ to $2\pi$):

$$H_z = \frac{I}{4\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{a^2}{R^3}d\phi = \frac{I\cdot 2\pi a^2}{4\pi(a^2+h^2)^{3/2}}$$
Result
$$\boxed{\bar{H} = \frac{Ia^2}{2(a^2+h^2)^{3/2}}\hat{a}_z}$$

Special cases:
• Centre of loop ($h=0$): $H = \dfrac{I}{2a}$ A/m
• Far field ($h\gg a$): $H \approx \dfrac{Ia^2}{2h^3} = \dfrac{m}{2\pi h^3}$ (dipole behavior, $m = I\pi a^2$)
